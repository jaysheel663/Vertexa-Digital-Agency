PROTOTYPE LEVELS

Choose the right level of fidelity

Not every idea requires a polished prototype.We select the level of detail according to the question that needs to be answered.

01

Concept Sketch

Quickly visualize an idea,workflow or information structure before investing in detailed design.

Best for:Early exploration
02

Wireframe

Establish page structure,navigation,hierarchy and key interactions without visual polish.

Best for:UX structure
03

Interactive Prototype

Connect important screens and interactions so users and stakeholders can experience a realistic flow.

Best for:Experience validation
04

High-Fidelity Prototype

Introduce visual design,realistic content,responsive behavior and refined interactions.

Best for:Final validation
