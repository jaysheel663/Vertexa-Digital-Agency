THE FRAMEWORK

Four research dimensions

Strong digital decisions require more than one source of information.We connect customer,business,market and technology perspectives.

01

Customer Research

Understand customer expectations,behaviors,frustrations,motivations,decision patterns and unmet needs.

  • User interviews
  • Journey analysis
  • Behavioral observations
  • Feedback analysis
02

Market Research

Study industry movement,competitor experiences,emerging opportunities and changing customer expectations.

  • Market landscape
  • Competitor review
  • Trend analysis
  • Opportunity mapping
03

Business Research

Connect research findings with organizational objectives,operational challenges and measurable business goals.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Business objectives
  • Process analysis
  • Capability mapping
04

Technology Research

Explore platforms,architecture,integrations and technologies that can support the future solution.

  • Technology landscape
  • Architecture review
  • Integration analysis
  • Technical feasibility
RESEARCH PROCESS

From questions to evidence

Research is structured as a progressive learning process rather than a single information-gathering activity.

01

Define

Identify the decisions,questions and assumptions that need investigation.

02

Discover

Collect information from customers,stakeholders,markets and systems.

03

Synthesize

Organize observations into themes,patterns and meaningful insights.

04

Prioritize

Separate important opportunities from assumptions that need more evidence.

05

Recommend

Translate evidence into practical product,experience and technology actions.
