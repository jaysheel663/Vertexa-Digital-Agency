AI PRODUCT DESIGN

Responsible AI Starts With Good Product Design

Useful

AI should solve a meaningful problem rather than exist simply because it is possible.

Understandable

Users should have a clear understanding of what the system is doing.

Human-Guided

Important decisions can include appropriate human review and control.

Context-Aware

Intelligent outputs should consider the user's situation and objective.

Measurable

AI products should have clear success criteria and feedback mechanisms.

Continuously Improved

Real-world usage should inform future product improvements.
