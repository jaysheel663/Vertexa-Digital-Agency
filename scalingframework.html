FOUR STAGES

A structured path to scale

Each stage focuses on a different type of readiness before the next level of investment and complexity.

01

Pilot

Prove the concept with a controlled audience,focused scope and clear learning goals.

  • Small scope
  • Focused audience
  • Defined hypothesis
02

Validate

Confirm customer value,technical reliability and operational feasibility.

  • Customer evidence
  • Technical testing
  • Operational review
03

Optimize

Remove friction,improve performance and prepare systems for larger demand.

  • Performance
  • Automation
  • Process refinement
04

Scale

Expand the solution across customers,markets,teams or business units.

  • Infrastructure
  • Governance
  • Continuous monitoring

90-DAY SCALE MODEL

Days 31–60

Improve

Strengthen systems, workflows, automation, monitoring and customer experience.

Days 61–90

Expand

Increase adoption gradually while measuring performance and learning continuously.
