JOURNEY THINKING

Every Touchpoint Shapes the Experience

A customer journey includes websites,applications,search,advertisements,support,onboarding,communication,products and post-purchase experiences.Connecting these touchpoints creates a more consistent customer relationship.

JOURNEY STAGES

Six Stages of the Customer Journey

01

Discover

Customers first become aware of a brand,product or service.Digital visibility and clear messaging are important here.

  • Search
  • Social media
  • Advertising
  • Content
02

Explore

Customers compare options and seek information that helps them understand whether the solution meets their needs.

  • Product pages
  • Reviews
  • Case studies
  • Resources
03

Decide

The experience should make it easy for customers to evaluate,trust and select the right option.

  • Pricing
  • Enquiry
  • Checkout
  • Consultation
04

Onboard

A smooth onboarding experience helps customers understand the product and reach their first meaningful outcome.

  • Account setup
  • Guidance
  • Training
  • Support
05

Use & Engage

Ongoing product experience,communication and support influence how customers perceive the value of the relationship.

  • Product usage
  • Notifications
  • Customer service
  • Personalization
06

Retain & Advocate

Strong experiences encourage customers to return and potentially recommend the product or service to others.

  • Retention
  • Loyalty
  • Feedback
  • Referrals
FRICTION MAPPING

Identify Where Customers Struggle

Confusing Navigation

Customers cannot easily find the information or action they need.

Slow Experiences

Performance problems create unnecessary friction during important interactions.

Complex Forms

Excessive fields or unclear instructions can reduce completion.

Disconnected Channels

Customers may receive inconsistent information across channels.
