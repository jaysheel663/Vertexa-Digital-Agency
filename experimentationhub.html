02/EXPERIMENT FRAMEWORK

A six-step learning loop.

01

Problem

Define what needs to improve.

02

Hypothesis

State what you believe will happen.

03

Prototype

Create the smallest useful version.

04

Measure

Define signals that matter.

05

Learn

Analyze the result and feedback.

06

Decide

Scale,change,pause or stop.
