STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

Five questions before execution

A clear strategy reduces confusion and helps project teams make consistent decisions as the work becomes more complex.

01

What business problem or opportunity are we addressing?

02

Who?

Which customers,employees or stakeholders are affected?

03

What?

What experience,product or capability should be created?

04

How?

What operating and technology model can deliver it?

05

When?

What should happen first,next and later?
