DISCOVERY FRAMEWORK

Our Discovery Process

01

Business Understanding

We understand the organization's goals,commercial model,current challenges,competitive position and future ambitions.

  • Business objectives
  • Revenue opportunities
  • Operational challenges
  • Strategic priorities
02

Stakeholder Discovery

We speak with the people who operate,manage and influence the product or service to understand different perspectives.

  • Leadership interviews
  • Team workshops
  • Process discussions
  • Expectation mapping
03

Customer Research

Customer needs,frustrations and expectations become an important input into experience and product decisions.

  • User interviews
  • Journey analysis
  • Feedback analysis
  • Behavior patterns
04

Digital Audit

We evaluate existing websites,products,campaigns,workflows,platforms,analytics and digital touchpoints.

  • UX audit
  • Performance review
  • Content review
  • Technology assessment
05

Market Intelligence

We study market movements and competitor experiences to identify opportunities and areas where the organization can differentiate.

  • Competitor analysis
  • Industry trends
  • Customer expectations
  • Emerging opportunities
06

Opportunity Mapping

Findings are transformed into a clear set of opportunities,problems and priorities that can guide the next stage.

  • Opportunity areas
  • Problem statements
  • Priority matrix
  • Strategic recommendations
