EXPERIMENT CANVAS

Turn Questions Into Testable Ideas

A structured experiment begins with a clear question and a measurable learning objective.

Problem

What problem are we trying to understand or solve?

Hypothesis

What do we believe will happen if the concept is introduced?

Audience

Which users,teams or stakeholders should participate?

Signal

What evidence would tell us that the idea is useful?

Success Condition

What measurable condition would support the next decision?

Learning Goal

What important uncertainty should the experiment reduce?
