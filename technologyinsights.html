DECISION FRAMEWORK

Build,Modernize or Replace?

Build

Consider building when the capability is strategically important and existing solutions do not meet the requirement.

Modernize

Modernize when an existing platform has valuable foundations but requires better architecture,performance or maintainability.

Replace

Replacement may be considered when technical constraints prevent the platform from meeting important future requirements.
