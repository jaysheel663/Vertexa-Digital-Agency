01

Digital Experience Transformation

Challenge

A growing organization had a fragmented digital presence.Customers struggled to understand services,navigate information and identify the right next step.

Context

Multiple pages and disconnected content had evolved over time without a unified experience structure.

Strategy

Vertexa mapped customer journeys,simplified information architecture and created a clearer content and conversion strategy.

Solution

A modern responsive website with structured navigation,focused landing pages,reusable components and clearer calls to action.

UX•Strategy•Design•Development
02

AI Customer Assistant

Challenge

A customer service team was spending significant time responding to repetitive questions and helping users locate information.

Context

Information existed across documents,support content and internal systems.

Strategy

Vertexa identified high-frequency questions and defined where conversational AI could safely support the customer journey.

Solution

An intelligent assistant experience combining guided conversations,knowledge retrieval and escalation paths.

AI • UX • Knowledge • Automation
03

Customer Portal

Challenge

Customers needed a centralized way to access information,submit requests,track progress and manage their relationship.

Context

Important actions were distributed across email,phone calls and multiple systems.

Strategy

The experience was reorganized around customer tasks rather than internal organizational structures.

Solution

A self-service platform connecting customer information,requests,documents,notifications and support.

Product•UX•Platform•Integration
